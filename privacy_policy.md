# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: June 12, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how the **Threads Enhancer** Chrome extension ("the Extension") handles user information and data.

## 1. Information Collection and Usage

**Threads Enhancer does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or browsing history.**

*   **No Personal Information:** The Extension does not collect personal identifiers, email addresses, names, or Threads credentials.
*   **No Tracking or Analytics:** We do not track your browsing activity, page views, or click history.
*   **No External Transmission:** All operations are performed locally in your browser. No data is sent to external servers or third-party services.

## 2. Local Storage and Preferences

The Extension uses Chrome's built-in `chrome.storage.sync` API to store and sync your settings (such as button visibility and layout preferences) across your signed-in browser instances.
*   This preference data remains entirely within the secure Google Chrome sandbox.
*   No personal or sensitive information is saved in this storage.

## 3. Scope of Permissions

*   **Host Permissions (`*://*.threads.net/*`, `*://*.threads.com/*`):** Used solely to inject the utility buttons (Open in New Tab, Copy Link) and the Back to Top button on the Threads website.
*   **Storage Permission:** Used exclusively to remember your custom display preferences for the injected buttons.

## 4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published directly in this repository and updated in the Chrome Web Store listing.

## 5. Contact

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the repository's issues page.
